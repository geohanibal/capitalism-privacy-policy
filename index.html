Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in the "Capitalism" mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a functional and synchronized gaming experience, we collect the following information:

  • Authentication Data: Your email address and username used for account creation and login via Firebase Authentication, Google Sign-In, or Facebook Login (whichever method you choose).
  • Age Verification: Your birth year, collected at sign-up to confirm you meet the minimum age requirement of 13.
  • Game Progress: Your in-game balance, owned assets, transaction history, and level progress are stored in Google Cloud Firestore. Your username and level may be visible to other players through the in-app friend search feature.
  • Purchase Data: If you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details — only confirmation that a purchase was completed, in order to credit your account.
  • Device Identifiers: AdMob may collect unique advertising IDs to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

2. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore)
  • AdMob
  • Google Sign-In
  • Facebook Login (Meta Platforms, Inc.)

If you sign up or log in using Facebook Login, Facebook may share your name, email address, and profile information with us, in accordance with the permissions you grant and Facebook's own Data Policy. You can manage or revoke this access at any time from your Facebook account's App Settings.

3. Children's Privacy

The "Capitalism" game is not intended for children under the age of 13. We collect your birth year at sign-up to verify this, and account creation is blocked for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your game data as long as your account is active. You have the right to delete your account at any time. You can do this directly through the Settings menu in the app. Deleting your account permanently erases your profile, progress, and all associated data from our database.

Facebook data deletion: If you signed up using Facebook Login and wish to request deletion of the data Facebook shared with us specifically, you may do so in either of two ways: (1) delete your account directly in the app's Settings menu, or (2) email us at sergi.qoniashvili@gmail.com with the subject "Facebook Data Deletion Request" including your in-game username or account email — we will confirm deletion within 30 days.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the data controller for the personal data described in this policy is Sergi Koniashvili, whose contact details are listed in Section 7. We process your data based on your consent (at sign-up) and our legitimate interest in operating and improving the game. You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate data;
  • Request erasure of your data (see Section 4);
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 7.

6. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable.
